### Key Contribution

**Save Draft Order Tool**  
Independently owned, designed, and developed a tool that lets sales reps build a cart on Merchology.com and convert it into a Shopify draft order. The workflow preserves pricing, fees, discounts, decoration selections, and cart details, replacing a slower manual process inside Shopify.

Stakeholders estimated this project alone could reduce sales-team order-entry time by roughly **23%**.
