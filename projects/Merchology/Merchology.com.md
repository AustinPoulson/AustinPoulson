[Merchology.com](https://www.merchology.com) is Merchology’s primary sales channel. It's built on Shopify as a highly customized storefront. My role sits between the business teams and the codebase: I take requests from sales, merchandising, ecommerce, accounting, and vendor partners, figure out what the site actually needs to do, then scope, build, test, and coordinate the work through release.

A large part of my work has been improving the ordering flow for internal sales teams. I've helped investigate and ship tools like the Save Draft Order button, which lets reps build a cart on the website and turn it into a Shopify draft order. This project was fully owned, designed and developed by me independently. The stakeholders involved estimated a 23% reduction of time spent by the sales department for this project alone.

I also work heavily on product customization and merchandising logic. That includes PDP changes for decoration methods, a la carte pricing, pad print fees, optional extras, logo locations, product recommendations, filtering, brand tiles, and product imagery. Much of this work is about turning real merchandising rules into clear, usable site behavior.

Beyond feature work, I help keep the storefront maintainable. I’ve worked on Shopify theme cleanup, release QA, deprecated code removal, customer logo/proofing bugs, discontinued-product redirects, canonical URL rollout checks, email template updates, and other fixes that keep the site reliable as the business changes.
